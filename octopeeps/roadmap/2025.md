# 2025

### Octo Kiddos 2.0

In October 2025 the OctoPeeps Kingdoms collection was relaunched as **Octo Kiddos 2.0**, with new artwork, metadata and staking pools. The original Octo Kiddos contract could no longer be administered, so Kingdoms became the home of the Kiddos line.

### Soft staking

Soft staking launched for Octo Kiddos 2.0, letting holders earn $FATH without ever moving their NFTs out of their wallet. A migration tool was added so hard stakers could move across in one action.

### $FATH pool funding

A live funding widget was added to the staking pages so the community could follow the $FATH reward pool in real time.

### Raffle Market

Query filters, active-state buttons and goal labels were added to the Raffle Market.
