# 2026

### Soulbound badges and smart wallets

Every Octo Kiddo 2.0 gained its own ERC-6551 smart wallet, and a soulbound badge system launched on top of it, claimed on-chain through a badge contract. The wallet dapp grew into a product of its own over the year: portfolio values, token swaps via ParaSwap, sending and gifting, a badge catalog, a leaderboard, a community activity feed and shareable profiles. See [Smart Wallets and Badges](../../utilities/smart-wallets-and-badges.md).

### $FATH on QuickSwap

$FATH liquidity went live on QuickSwap as a FATH / POL pair, making the token freely tradable for the first time. A dedicated $FATH page was added to the website.

### Mint NFTs with $FATH

Octo Kiddos 2.0 became mintable with $FATH as well as POL, giving the token a direct sink.

### Bocto Token Tools and $FATH subscriptions

Bocto added $FATH as a payment option for subscriptions in March 2026, alongside a Token Tools suite: tipping, rain, airdrops and raffles run straight from Discord, with payouts to verified external wallets. The Guard plugin gained cross-server user flagging.

### Octopin

We launched [Octopin](../../products/octopin.md), our own IPFS pinning service for NFT communities, and moved OctoPeeps metadata onto it. It serves collection creators real IPFS storage with unlimited bandwidth on every tier and a free plan that covers around 2,000 NFTs.

### The Play page and on-chain casino

A games hub launched at octopeeps.com/play with FATH Loot Cave, Coin Toss, Dice Den, Fortune Wheel and Derby Dash, plus a POL Fortune Wheel.

### Social mint

A free mint route opened up: complete X and Discord tasks, earn points, and claim an Octo Kiddo 2.0 without paying.

### Kiddo Profiles

Customisable public profiles for every Kiddo, with a recent gallery and dynamic social previews.

### Peep Duel

A daily community vote landed in Discord: two Kiddos head to head, the server picks a winner, and past duels are readable on the web.

### Raid Boss

A community boss hunt launched in Discord in August: automatic spawns, button combat with cooldowns, crits and misses, bosses that fight back, and loot in POL, $FATH and NFTs. It kept growing through the autumn with mutations, omens, daily training, curses and blessings, downed-and-revive, seasonal bosses, secret rares and Abyssal Shards. See [Raid Boss](../../utilities/raid-boss.md).

### The $FATH endgame

In September 2026 the staking rewards programme reached its 100,000,000 mint cap and was closed in an orderly way. A final snapshot was taken, 22,507,834.46 $FATH was airdropped to 161 wallets, the remaining unminted supply was burned, and contract ownership was renounced. $FATH supply is now permanently final.
