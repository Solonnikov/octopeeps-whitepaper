# 2027

### A finished collection

The community voted to cap Octo Kiddos 2.0 at 2,222 rather than run it to the contract maximum. Once the final mint event closes, minting is switched off permanently and the collection is complete.

### Building on fixed foundations

With $FATH supply final and the collections closed, the focus moves from issuing to building: deeper gameplay in Raid Boss and the casino, more badges to earn, and more places to spend $FATH across the ecosystem.

### Bocto and Octopin

Both products keep growing as businesses in their own right: more plugins and supported chains for Bocto, more capacity and tooling for Octopin.

### A new collection, a new chain

We are exploring a new OctoPeeps collection beyond Polygon - on Solana or another chain - to bring the universe to a new audience. The existing collections stay exactly as they are: finished, capped and unchanged.
