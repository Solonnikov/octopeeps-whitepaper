# 2022

### Phase 1: Q1 2022

#### The Beginning

* Setting up social media accounts (Instagram, Twitter, Discord, Telegram, TikTok).
* Promote our social media profiles and gain an audience.
* Building a cohesive community with the same vision and love for NFTs, art, and the crypto world.
* Public release of the first 1,000 collection. The whole collection will be minted at once on the Polygon blockchain.
* Whitelisting our community for the next collection release through contests and giveaways.
* The rest of the collection will be listed on OpenSea at a minimum Opensea listing price.

### Phase 2: Q2 2022

#### Development

* Start building a minting website for the next generation of OctoPeeps.
* Drop OctoPeeps Cartoon Club collection in several phases and themes (Simspons, Superheroes, Rock & Pop Stars) by 30 NFTs of each.
* Each holder of OctoPeeps NFT will automatically get whitelisted for the Phase 3 whitelist FREE Mint.
* Fill out the whitelist with 100 members for future pre-sale. At this stage, you can get whitelisted for 5 invites or boost our Discord server.

### Phase 3: Q3 2022

#### Growth

* Collaborations and Giveaways with other artists from the Community
* Holders Giveaway of 1 NFT from OctoPeeps Cartoon Club collection
* Design a new generation of OctoPeeps collection with brand new appearance and rarity levels. The collection will consist of 4848 NTFs.
* Release minting website and start whitelist-sale for community members. Whitelisted price - FREE. Pre-sale price - 0.07 ETH. Public sale price - 0.01 ETH.

### Phase 4: Q4 2022

#### Fun & Giving back

* All OctoPeeps Holders will receive the status "Partner" and other benefits. Each holder of OctoPeeps will receive NFTs from the next collection phase for free.
* Launch marketing campaigns and collaborate with other NFT projects.
* 15% of all initial sales will be sent back to the holders. The more NFTs you hold - the more you will get back. Another 15% will be donated to the environmental charity organization - Oceanic Society.
* Give Away Party! We will gift to our 10 random Community members 10 legendary 3D and Voxel OctoPeeps.
* Launching OctoPeeps token and NFT staking system.
* Release of Roadmap 2023.
