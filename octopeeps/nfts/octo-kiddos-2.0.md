# Octo Kiddos 2.0

Octo Kiddos 2.0 is the live OctoPeeps collection on Polygon.

Contract: `0xa657e5e2Ef8AeC8f54D5E136fec40F296425881a`

This collection was originally deployed as OctoPeeps Kingdoms. In October 2025 it was relaunched as Octo Kiddos 2.0 with new artwork, metadata and staking pools.

The reason is on-chain and permanent: ownership of the original Octo Kiddos contract was renounced, so no one can administer it any more - no minting, no pausing, no metadata updates, ever. That collection is frozen exactly as it stands. Rather than leave the Kiddos line stranded, the Kingdoms contract became its home.

So the contract still reports its original name internally, while the collection everyone knows is Octo Kiddos 2.0. The original [Octo Kiddos](octo-kiddos.md) remains a separate, finished collection.

### Supply

A community vote is under way on capping Octo Kiddos 2.0 at 2,222 rather than letting it run to the contract's maximum. The contract's own `maxSupply` reads 4,848 and can never be changed, so a cap would be enforced by closing the mint permanently once 2,222 is reached.

### Smart wallets and badges

Every Octo Kiddo 2.0 has its own ERC-6551 smart wallet that can hold NFTs and tokens, and earns soulbound badges that stay with the Kiddo forever.
