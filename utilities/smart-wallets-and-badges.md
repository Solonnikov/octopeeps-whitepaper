# Smart Wallets and Badges

Every Octo Kiddo 2.0 has its own wallet. Not a wallet you own that holds the NFT - a wallet that the NFT itself owns, using the **ERC-6551** token-bound account standard. Sell the Kiddo and everything inside it goes with it.

<https://octopeeps.com/dapps/tba>

### What a Kiddo's wallet can do

* **Hold assets** - NFTs, POL, $FATH, USDC, whatever you send it
* **Swap tokens** inside the wallet, via ParaSwap, with price impact shown before you confirm
* **Send** tokens out, with balance validation and a MAX button
* **Gift** the whole wallet by transferring the Kiddo
* Show a live **portfolio value** in USD

### Badges

Your Kiddo earns **soulbound badges** - permanent, on-chain proof of what you have done in the ecosystem. They are claimed on-chain through a badge contract that verifies the requirement against your wallet, and once claimed they cannot be transferred out. They stay with the Kiddo forever.

Unearned badges show as placeholders in the catalog, so you can see what there is to go after.

Example: the **FATH Millionaire** badge, for holding 1,000,000 $FATH or more.

### The community layer

The wallets are not isolated. There is a directory of every activated Kiddo wallet with search and pagination, a leaderboard, a community activity feed covering transfers and badge claims, and shareable public profiles you can post to X.

### Kiddo Profiles

On top of the wallet, each Kiddo has a customisable public profile with a bio and a recent gallery, plus dynamic social previews so a shared profile renders properly. Profiles feed a discovery page and get spotlighted in Discord.
