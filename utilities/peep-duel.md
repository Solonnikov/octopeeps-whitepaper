# Peep Duel

Peep Duel is a daily community vote in Discord. Every day the bot posts two NFTs side by side - one Octo Kiddo against one Octo Kiddo 2.0 - and you vote for your pick with a button. The next day it announces the winner and the final score.

It exists to give more Kiddos their moment in the spotlight and to give the server something small to do every day. One random voter from the winning side may get a community reward from the team.

Duels are also readable on the web, so past matchups, scores and winners are not locked inside Discord.

Click **Notify Me** under a duel post to get pinged for future ones.
