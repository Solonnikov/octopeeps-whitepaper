# Raffle Market

Win and buy exclusive NFTs using the ERC-20 $FATH token.

Fathom Raffle Market is the place where you can buy and win NFTs for our native Fathom token ($FATH).

**What is a raffle market?**

A raffle market is a platform where users can purchase raffle tickets for the chance to win an NFT prize. The more tickets a user purchases, the higher their chances of winning.

**What are the different items available in the raffle market?**

Currently, we offer four different item types: ERC-721 NFTs, ERC-1155 NFTs, Raffle tickets for NFTs, and Whitelist spots.

**How do I participate in a raffle market?**

To participate in our raffle market, you need to connect to the raffle DApp. Once you've done it, you can browse the available raffles and purchase tickets for the ones you're interested in.

**How do I buy raffle tickets?**

Tickets are bought with $FATH. Staking has ended, but $FATH is still earned by beating the Raid Boss in Discord, winning in the on-chain casino, and through community giveaways and campaigns. It can also be bought on QuickSwap. See [$FATH](usdfath.md).

**How are winners chosen in a raffle market?**

Winners are chosen randomly using an algorithm that selects a winning ticket number on a Blockchain when all the tickets for a specific item are sold out or it can be done anytime by the decision of the team.

**What happens if I win a raffle?**

If you win a raffle, you will be sent your prize to the wallet you provided. You can check the results of each raffle in the "Raffle Results" section.
