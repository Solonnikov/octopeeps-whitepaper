# Raid Boss

Raid Boss is our automated boss-fight game in Discord. A boss spawns on its own at a random time, the whole server fights it together over a couple of days, and it drops loot when it dies.

No wallet connection, no gas, no transaction to join. You fight with buttons in a Discord channel.

### How a fight works

A boss appears in the raid channel with its own art and a health bar. Everyone attacks it using three abilities, each on its own cooldown:

* ⚔️ **Hit** - fast, short cooldown
* 🏹 **Double Shot** - harder, medium cooldown
* 💥 **Skill Attack** - the heavy one, long cooldown

Attacks can crit, and they can miss. The boss fights back: it can wound you, which slows how fast your abilities recharge. As its health drops it moves through states - healthy, damaged, enraged - and its art changes with it.

If nobody finishes it in time, the boss escapes and nobody gets the loot.

### Loot

Every slot rolls independently, and any of them can roll nothing:

* **POL** to the Slayer, whoever lands the killing blow
* **$FATH** to the top damage dealers
* **An Octo Kiddo NFT** to a random fighter who landed at least three hits

Rewards are paid out by hand from the community treasury. The bot rolls the loot and announces winners; it holds no keys and moves no funds on its own.

### Abyssal Shards

Every fighter earns shards when a boss goes down, based on damage dealt, with a floor for anyone who landed three or more hits and extra for the Slayer and the top three. If the boss escapes you still keep half.

Check yours with `/shards`.

### Deeper mechanics

The game has grown well past hit-the-boss:

* **Mutations** - bosses can spawn mutated, with the art overlaid to match
* **Omens** - signs during the wait before a spawn
* **Training** - `/train` between hunts builds daily readiness, which sharpens both your damage and your aim
* **Curses and blessings** - drops and debuffs that change how a fight plays out
* **Downed and revive** - go down in a fight and the crew can bring you back
* **Seasons** - seasonal bosses with their own named seasons and art
* **Secret bosses** - rare ones that only turn up occasionally, including a community-requested one
