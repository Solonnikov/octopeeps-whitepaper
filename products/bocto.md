# Bocto

Bocto is our Discord bot for web3 communities. It delivers automated NFT updates, secure token-gated roles and smart moderation across multiple blockchains and marketplaces, including OpenSea and MagicEden. The NFT Tracker posts mints, sales and listings, and on OpenSea it also posts offers with wallet signals that flag likely bots, so holders can see lowball bot offers for what they are.

It is a product of the OctoPeeps team, and **$FATH is accepted for subscriptions**.

### Bocto Pro

One subscription per server unlocks the Pro tier of every Bocto plugin, for **$9 a month or $90 a year**. Pay by card, or in crypto including $FATH, for a month or up to a year at a time. Everything that was free stays free. Pro servers can also give the bot their own name and avatar, shown on every message it posts in that server.

### Token Tools

Bocto also runs community payouts straight from Discord: tipping, rain, airdrops and raffles, with payouts to verified external wallets. Free on every plan, server admins can also switch on `/price` for any coin's price and `/gas` for what a transfer costs on each chain right now, shown privately or shared in the channel. The Guard plugin adds cross-server user flagging on top of moderation.

### Personal Bocto

Bocto is not only for servers. Anyone can add Bocto to their own Discord account and use the `/bocto` command in any server, DM or group chat, even where Bocto is not installed. The first personal feature is the wallet: check your balance, get your deposit address, tip anyone and withdraw to your own wallet. A person who is tipped for the first time gets a Claim button, and no wallet is created and no funds move until they click it. You can also look up any coin's price, 24h change, market cap and volume with `/bocto price`, and see what a simple transfer costs on each supported chain right now with `/bocto gas`. With `/bocto watch` Bocto keeps an eye on one of your verified EVM wallets on Ethereum, Base, Polygon, BNB Chain and Arbitrum, and sends you a DM when tokens, NFTs or coins arrive. You choose what to watch and a minimum value, and spam tokens are filtered out. More personal tools will follow.

### Task Manager

A task board for small teams, right inside Discord. Create, assign and complete tasks with slash commands or from the dashboard, and Bocto posts every update to your task channel. Free servers can add 2 team members, Bocto Pro raises that to 10.
