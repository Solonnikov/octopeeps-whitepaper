# Bocto Gate

Token gating for Telegram, on any chain and any platform.

Bocto Gate protects a Telegram group by checking what people actually hold or subscribe to. Verification takes seconds and costs no gas, because nothing is written on-chain - it only reads.

**What it can gate on:**

* Token and NFT ownership on **EVM chains, Solana and Bitcoin**
* **Patreon** pledge tiers
* **Twitch** subscriptions

Setup is three steps in Telegram itself. No code, no dashboard.

There is a free tier, and paid plans are billed with Telegram Stars or USDT via CryptoBot.
