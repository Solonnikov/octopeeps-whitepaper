# Bocto VPN

A Telegram bot that sells and manages VPN subscriptions.

Everything happens inside Telegram: pick a plan, pay, and get your connection details back in the chat. Payment is by **Telegram Stars** or **USDT** via CryptoBot, so no card and no account signup is needed.

Available in English, Spanish and Portuguese.
